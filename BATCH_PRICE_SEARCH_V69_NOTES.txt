Tahalil Alakkad V69 — Batch Price Search
========================================

Changes on top of V68:
1. HBsAg / HBs Ag / HBs-Ag resolve to the same HBs Ag catalogue test.
   The compact-Latin exact matcher also supports similar spacing/punctuation variants
   for other English medical abbreviations without relaxing short-abbreviation fuzzy rules.
2. Explicit exact searches collapse to the exact requested test instead of showing
   related substring results (for example Creatinine resolves directly to Creatinine).
3. Multi-line price inquiry keeps every line as a separate requested test.
4. Price inquiry shows a direct summary card with the number of resolved tests and
   the combined customer-price total. No Add-to-List action is required in price inquiry.
5. Unmatched tests remain visible as not found and are not included in the total.
