V12 - USER SETTINGS + PRIVATE CUSTOMER STATUS

1) Regular users now have a Settings button.
2) Regular-user settings are limited to personal options:
   - App lock on/off
   - Unlock with phone biometric/device credential OR a custom 4-6 digit app PIN
   - Change app PIN
   - Auto-lock: immediately / 1 min / 5 min / 15 min
   - Dark mode
   - Font size: small / medium / large
   - Show English / Arabic / market test names
3) Personal settings are stored per signed-in Firebase account on the device.
4) Manager keeps full manager settings and manager dashboard.
5) Blacklist and archived/deleted customer tabs are completely hidden from non-manager UI.
6) Non-manager customer list is always limited to active customers only.
7) Manager-only blacklist/archive/restore controls remain unchanged.
8) App PIN is not stored as plain text; a salted SHA-256 verifier is stored locally.
9) versionCode = 16.
10) Existing Firestore rules remain compatible; no rule change is required for this UI release.
