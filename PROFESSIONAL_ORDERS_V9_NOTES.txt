V9 Professional Orders
- Professional checkout before saving customer orders.
- Automatic order number and date.
- Discount, payment status, paid/remaining balance, and notes.
- Order history shows payment state and financial summary.
- Order details show subtotal, discount, final total, paid and remaining.
- Saved orders can be shared as a PDF with order/customer/payment details.
