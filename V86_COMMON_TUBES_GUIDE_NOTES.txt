V86 COMMON TUBES GUIDE

- Test Guide now prioritizes only common routine tube colors.
- Main visible palette: Gold SST, Lavender EDTA, Light Blue Citrate, Gray Fluoride, Green Heparin, Black ESR when applicable.
- Rare specialty colors such as Royal Blue and Pink are no longer shown as routine visual guidance.
- Specialty tests display a neutral special-tube instruction and direct staff to the lab SOP.
- V85 stability/security/commercial features retained.
