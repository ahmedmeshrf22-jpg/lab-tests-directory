V137 Auto Backup
- Daily local encrypted backup scheduled for 04:00 device local time.
- Exact Alarm is used when Android grants Alarms & reminders access; JobScheduler fallback stays near 04:00 otherwise.
- Automatic files are saved to Download/Tahalil Alakkad/Backups as .tahbak.
- The password from the next successful manual backup is encrypted with Android Keystore and reused for automatic backups.
- Backup start/completion/failure notifications added.
- Restore start/completion/failure notifications added.
- Firebase Rules and data schema are unchanged.
