V72 — Test Information Guide / Hologram

- Adds a "المزيد — دليل التحليل" button under test cards.
- Opens a full-screen hologram-style educational panel.
- Shows: what the test is, when/why it is ordered, specimen type, tube name, cap color, illustrated tube, preparation, and approximate turnaround time.
- Medical content is educational and source-labelled.
- Dedicated source links are used for common tests where available (MedlinePlus / CDC).
- Blood collection guidance references WHO phlebotomy guidance and BD Vacutainer tube specifications.
- Tube color is never presented as a substitute for the laboratory SOP; special/variable tests are explicitly flagged.
