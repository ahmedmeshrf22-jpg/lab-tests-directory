Tahalil Alakkad V38 - Optional Output + QR Customer Search

Version: 4.14-optional-output-qr-v38 (versionCode 43)

1) Saving an order no longer creates or sends PDF files automatically.
2) After a successful save, the user can close with Save Only.
3) Optional outputs are available independently for Customer and Lab:
   - PDF
   - PNG image
4) Customer receipt keeps patient contact data, unique customer QR, prices/totals and clinic footer.
5) Lab request keeps patient name, age, gender, date and English test names without prices.
6) New V2 QR payload is immutable per customer because it uses Firestore customer document ID only.
7) Old V1 QR codes remain readable because QR lookup accepts the shared prefix and resolves ID/FILE fields.
8) Customer Records has Search by customer QR button and helper text. Valid QR opens the customer profile directly.
9) PNG is rendered from the same PDF layout so PDF and image stay visually identical.
