Tahalil Alakkad V112 - Simple Clinic Order Flow

Goal:
Make the clinic order flow obvious and remove workflow clutter.

Clinic order flow:
1. Choose/register customer.
2. Choose tests.
3. Continue to payment.
4. Press one clear button: "حفظ وإرسال الطلب للمعمل".
5. The order is saved with workflow_status = sent_to_lab and appears automatically to the lab.
6. Clinic does not manually advance lab workflow stages.

Clinic order tracking:
- All
- Sent to Lab
- Processing
- Result Ready
The clinic sees status only; the lab controls operational progress.

Lab screen remains:
- طلبات جديدة
- جاري التنفيذ
- النتائج المرسلة

Firebase data model remains compatible with V111 and Windows V111 FIX2.
