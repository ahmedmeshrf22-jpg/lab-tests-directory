V25 - Readable input fields

- Increased minimum height for all text-entry fields across the app.
- Single-line fields: minimum 64dp.
- Multi-line fields: minimum 72-96dp.
- Applied to login, PIN, customer forms, reports, admin/users, manager settings, search, pricing and order fields.
- Prevents typed Arabic/English text and labels from appearing vertically clipped, including with larger in-app font size.
- All V24 and earlier functionality is preserved.
