V87 — Non-laboratory smart fallback

- Any search term not found in the laboratory catalogue is never given a fake lab price.
- Known non-laboratory items are classified locally/offline: X-ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound, Doppler, ECG, Echo, endoscopy, biopsy, and likely medication/dose items.
- PXR Urinary Tract is specifically recognized as a plain X-ray of the urinary tract / KUB.
- Each unmatched item shows its likely category, a short medical explanation, and a confidence-safe message.
- Unknown items stay explicitly "unclassified" instead of being guessed.
- External web search is available from the unmatched item card.
- Before opening the browser the app warns clearly that the user is leaving Tahalil Alakkad for an external website and offers Continue / Cancel.
- Image/PDF smart reading now keeps explicitly requested non-laboratory investigations so recognizable items can also appear as unmatched medical items in the normal search UI.
