V11 CUSTOMER CONTROL
- versionCode 15
- Manager-only customer blacklist controls.
- Optional blacklist reason.
- Blacklisted customers are excluded from new-order customer selection.
- Safe customer removal uses archive instead of destructive delete, preserving order history.
- Customer list has Active / Blacklist / Deleted sections with counters.
- Archived customers can be restored by the manager.
- Blacklisted customers can be unblocked by the manager.
- New Firestore rules file: FIRESTORE_RULES_V11.txt.
