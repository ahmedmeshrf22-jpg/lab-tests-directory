V32 - Data Integrity & Conflict Protection

- Manager Data Integrity screen inside Administration.
- Scans customers for duplicate normalized phone ownership.
- Scans up to 1500 orders for missing identifiers, duplicate order numbers, duplicate operation IDs, totals/payment inconsistencies.
- Detects possible customer edit conflicts from recent audit activity by different users.
- Online customer/order/payment edits perform a server freshness check and reject stale writes before overwriting newer data.
- Every audit entry gets a unique operation_id; new orders and payments also store stable operation IDs.
- Safe repair can recalculate order totals/payment status without deleting customer/order data.
- Critical integrity findings feed the manager alert center after a scan.
- Keeps V31 Smart Admin Alerts, V30 System Health, V29 performance, V28 language/offline sync and earlier security/navigation/UI fixes.
